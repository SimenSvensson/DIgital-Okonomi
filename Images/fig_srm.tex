\caption{Stakeholder Relationship Model.
Double-bordered boxes represent stakeholder groups,
which are not necessarily sides of the core trading platform.
Grey arrows show relationships, and red arrows show
network effects using the SRM notation of
\citet[Sect.~19.3]{OverbyAudestad2021}.
Buyers and sellers are the core trading sides.
Data customers use an ancillary service,
futures traders participate in complementary markets,
and Small Participants are a tariff segment
within the trading sides.
The one-way informational benefit of trading activity
for data customers is marked (+/0).}
\label{fig:srm}
