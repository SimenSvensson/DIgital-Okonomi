\section*{Declaration of AI use}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Declaration of AI use}

AI use is required in this course and is documented in Appendices~A and~B. We used Claude (Anthropic; model \texttt{claude-opus-5-5}; Claude desktop app) in three ways. First, to search for and verify sources. Second, to generate the raw outputs reproduced in Appendix~B. Third, to produce a first draft of the report text, tables and figures. The group reviewed, corrected and rewrote this draft, and checked every factual claim and reference against the original sources. AI tools are not cited as sources for any fact. The NTNU declaration form for AI use is submitted separately.
