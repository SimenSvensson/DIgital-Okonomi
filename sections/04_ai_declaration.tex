\section*{Declaration of AI use}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Declaration of AI use}

AI use is required in this course and is documented in Appendices~A and~B. Before prompting AI for each task, the group formulated its own initial ideas and analytical points using the assignment requirements and course concepts. We then used Claude (Anthropic; model \texttt{claude-opus-5-5}; Claude desktop app) to assist with source research, generate the task-specific outputs reproduced in Appendix~B, and produce the tables and figures from the group's own sketches.

We compared the AI outputs with our initial ideas and checked them against course theory and case sources. We then revised the analysis. Appendix~A records the identified errors, unsupported assumptions and subsequent corrections. The original task-specific AI outputs are preserved unedited in Appendix~B. 

We also used ChatGPT (OpenAI; model GPT 6.1 Sol) to convert the group's own written revisions into LaTeX code, including the code for two figures, and to commit them to the project repository. The content and wording of these revisions are the group's own. Claude was later used in the same way to convert the group's main text from Word into LaTeX. These uses, and the production of tables and figures, are recorded in Appendix~A.6. AI tools are not cited as evidence for factual claims. The NTNU declaration form for AI use accompanies the submission.
