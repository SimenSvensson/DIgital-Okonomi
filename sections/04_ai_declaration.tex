\section*{Declaration of AI use}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Declaration of AI use}

AI use is required in this course and is documented in Appendices~A and~B. Before prompting AI for each task, the group formulated its own initial ideas and analytical points using the assignment requirements and course concepts. We then used Claude (Anthropic; model \texttt{claude-opus-5-5}; Claude desktop app) to assist with source research, generate the task-specific outputs reproduced in Appendix~B, and support the development of draft text, tables and figures.

The group compared the AI outputs with its own initial reasoning, evaluated them against course theory and case evidence, and revised the analysis. Appendix~A records the identified errors, unsupported assumptions and subsequent corrections. The original task-specific AI outputs are preserved unedited in Appendix~B.

We also used Codex (OpenAI desktop app) for a supplementary review against the assignment requirements and for proposed corrections to the description of our working process, the system-price dependency and the classification of platform sides. This additional use is recorded in Appendix~A. The group is responsible for reviewing the proposed revisions and for the arguments and wording submitted. AI tools are not cited as evidence for factual claims. The NTNU declaration form for AI use accompanies the submission.
