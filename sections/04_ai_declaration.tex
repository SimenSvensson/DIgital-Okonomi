\section*{Declaration of AI use}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Declaration of AI use}

AI use is required in this course and is documented in Appendices~A and~B. Before prompting AI for each task, the group formulated its own initial ideas and analytical points using the assignment requirements and course concepts. We then used Claude (Anthropic; model \texttt{claude-opus-5-5}; Claude desktop app) to assist with source research, generate the task-specific outputs reproduced in Appendix~B, and support the development of draft text, tables and figures.

The group compared the AI outputs with its own initial reasoning, evaluated them against course theory and case evidence, and revised the analysis. Appendix~A records the identified errors, unsupported assumptions and subsequent corrections. Corrections to the AI-drafted report text are logged in Appendix~A.7. The original task-specific AI outputs are preserved unedited in Appendix~B. After these corrections, the group reworded the AI-drafted main text (Sections~1--3) by hand. The rewording changed the formulation, not the analysis, the sources or the corrections recorded in Appendix~A.

We also used Codex (OpenAI desktop app) to convert the group's own written revisions into LaTeX code, including the code for two figures, and to commit them to the project repository. The content and wording of these revisions are the group's own. Claude was later used in the same way to convert the group's reworded main text from Word into LaTeX. Both uses are recorded in Appendix~A.6. AI tools are not cited as evidence for factual claims. The NTNU declaration form for AI use accompanies the submission.
